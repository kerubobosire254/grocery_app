## The idea

Stocked is a personal monthly shopping planner for anyone who has ever stood in a supermarket aisle thinking, "There was something else I needed. What was it?"

Add things as they cross your mind. Pop in a rough price. Tick items off as they land in your trolley. By the time you shop, the list is already done.

> **Think of it. Add it. Forget about it. Buy it later.**

---

## What Stocked can do

| Plan | Track | Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Build a list for the whole month | Attach estimated prices | Install it on your phone like a real app |
| Add items the moment you remember them | Tick things off as you buy | Keep your data saved on your own device |
| Delete items you no longer need | See estimated, bought and remaining totals | Print the list when you want it on paper |
| Sort everything into categories | Watch your spending take shape | Works with no account and no login |

## Put it on your phone

Stocked is a Progressive Web App (PWA), so there is no app store and no download queue.

1. Open the [Stocked web app](https://kerubobosire254.github.io/grocery_app/) on your phone.
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App** in your browser menu.
3. Open it from your home screen like any other app.

## The stack

| Layer | Tech | What it does here |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Structure | HTML | Lays out the list, categories and totals |
| Style | CSS | Keeps it clean and responsive on any screen |
| Logic | JavaScript | Runs the list and does the maths |
| Storage | Local Storage | Saves your list in your browser |
| Install | PWA and service workers | Makes it installable and ready to open like an app |
| Hosting | GitHub Pages | Serves the live app for free |

No account. No login. No backend. Just your list.

---

## How it works

JavaScript manages the shopping list and calculates your totals. Everything is saved in your browser, so your list is right where you left it next time you open the app on the same device.

## Why I built it

I wanted a shopping list that actually fits how I shop.

Halfway through the month I remember something I need, tell myself I will definitely remember it later, and then shopping day arrives and my brain has quietly deleted half the list.

Stocked gives me one place to drop things all month long.
